# Changelog

## [Unreleased] - 2026-06-03
### Added
- **100% Offline Prayer Calculation**: Integrated `adhan.js` to calculate prayer times locally, supporting Hanafi/Shafi and major calculation authorities without an internet connection.
- **Panch Surah & 99 Names**: Added offline reading for Yaseen, Mulk, Waqiah, Kahf, and Asma-ul-Husna.
- **Zakat Calculator**: Added a comprehensive tool in the Profile section to calculate 2.5% Zakat on eligible wealth.
- **Qibla Map Fallback**: Added a free Leaflet map showing the geodesic line to the Kaaba when compass calibration is difficult.

### Changed
- **Modals & UI**: Updated all popups to drop down from the top. Enhanced glassmorphism transparency for a more premium look across both Light and Dark themes.
